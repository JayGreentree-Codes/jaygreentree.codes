Modern web development often overcomplicates simple personal websites. When all you need to share is your writing, selected projects, and a resume, a database-driven stack adds unnecessary maintenance.

## Why Static Works

With Hugo and Markdown, your entire website lives in plain text files inside Git:

1. **Instant Builds:** Hundreds of pages compile in milliseconds.
2. **Portable Content:** Your articles and case studies aren't locked inside a proprietary database schema.
3. **Zero Security Patches:** Serving static HTML files eliminates server-side vulnerabilities.

Paired with Tailwind CSS v4's native Hugo integration, styling stays clean, maintainable, and fast.
